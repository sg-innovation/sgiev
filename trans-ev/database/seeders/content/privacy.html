1. Who we are

{{company_legal_name}} (“Trans EV”, “we”) operates transevrecharge.com and the Trans EV charging network. We are the Data Fiduciary for personal data collected through the Services under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”). This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it and the choices and rights you have.

2. Personal data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, mobile number and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Booking data: station, charger, date and time, vehicle make, model, registration number, battery capacity and charge levels, and your acceptance of station rules.
  • Billing data: billing name, state, optional GSTIN, payment status, amounts, payment reference numbers and invoices. Card, UPI and bank credentials are collected and processed only by our payment partner.
  • Location data: your device location, only when you tap “Near me”. It is used in your browser and in that single search to sort stations by distance; we do not store your location history.
  • Support data: messages you send through the contact form, email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information and security logs generated when you use the website.

3. Why we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and let you log in.
  • To take, manage and confirm bookings, and to operate the charging session.
  • To process payments, issue receipts, GST tax invoices and credit notes, and handle refunds.
  • To send booking confirmations, reminders and important service messages.
  • To respond to your queries and complaints.
  • To keep the Services safe, prevent fraud and misuse, and investigate incidents.
  • To meet legal obligations, including tax, accounting, audit and cyber-security reporting requirements.

4. Consent and legitimate uses

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you create an account, book or contact us, and for the legitimate uses permitted by Section 7 of the DPDP Act, such as complying with law, responding to legal requests and handling safety emergencies. You may withdraw consent at any time (see section 9); this does not affect processing already carried out or data we must keep by law.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as needed with:

  • Payment partners (RBI-authorised payment aggregators and banks) to process payments and refunds;
  • Service providers who host our systems, send emails and SMS/WhatsApp messages, and provide accounting software, under contracts that require them to protect your data and use it only on our instructions;
  • Station partners and staff, limited to booking details needed to serve you at the station;
  • Government and regulators, including tax authorities and CERT-In, when required by law.

6. Where and how we protect your data

  • Your data is stored on servers located in India.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and sensitive fields are encrypted at rest.
  • Access is limited by role, protected with multi-factor authentication for staff, and recorded in audit logs.
  • We regularly test our systems for security weaknesses. If a personal data breach occurs we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected users as required by law.

7. How long we keep data

  • Invoices, payments and accounting records: at least 8 years, as required by GST and company law.
  • Booking records and rule acceptances: for the same period, as they support invoices and dispute resolution.
  • Security and system logs: at least 180 days, as required by CERT-In directions.
  • Account and profile data: while your account is active. When you delete your account we erase or anonymise it, except where the law requires us to keep it.
  • Support messages: up to 3 years after the query is closed.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • obtain a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • correct, complete or update your data (most details can be edited in your account);
  • request erasure of data that is no longer needed, subject to legal retention requirements;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity;
  • raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied.

9. Withdrawing consent and deleting your account

You can delete your account from Profile settings or write to {{support_email}}. Withdrawing consent means we can no longer provide bookings to you. Records we must keep by law are retained only for that purpose.

10. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in, protect forms against misuse (CSRF protection) and remember preferences such as light or dark mode. We do not use advertising cookies. Map tiles are loaded from a mapping provider, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

11. Children

The Services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly create accounts for, or process the personal data of, anyone under 18 without verifiable parental consent.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page with a new “last updated” date and, for significant changes, notify you by email or on the website.

13. Grievance officer and contact

For questions, requests to exercise your rights, or complaints about how we handle personal data, contact our Grievance Officer. We respond within 30 days.
