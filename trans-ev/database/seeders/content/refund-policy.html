1. Overview

This policy explains how cancellations, refunds and billing adjustments work for bookings made on transevrecharge.com. It forms part of our Terms of Service. All times are Indian Standard Time and are measured against the start time of your booked slot.

2. Reserved but unpaid slots

A reserved slot is held for {{booking_hold_minutes}} minutes while you pay. If you cancel it, or the hold expires before payment, nothing is charged and no refund is needed.

3. Cancellations by you (paid bookings)

{{refund_tiers_table}}

You can cancel from My bookings in your account. The refund amount is shown before you confirm the cancellation.

4. Cancellations or problems on our side

You receive a full refund, or a free reschedule if you prefer, when:

  • the station or your charger is offline, under maintenance or closed at your booked time;
  • the charger cannot start a session with a compatible vehicle because of a fault in our equipment;
  • we cancel your booking for operational or safety reasons.

If charging stops part-way because of a fault on our side, you are charged only for the energy delivered and the rest is refunded.

5. Billing adjustments after charging

  • You pay an estimate when you book. Where a station bills on actual energy delivered, if your vehicle takes less energy than estimated the difference is refunded automatically after the session.
  • The booking fee is refunded only in the cases in sections 3 (full refund) and 4.
  • Idle fees incurred after charging ends are not refundable.

6. Failed, pending or duplicate payments

If money is debited but your booking is not confirmed, or you are charged twice for the same booking, the amount is refunded automatically. If you do not see it within the timelines below, contact us with your booking code and payment reference.

7. How and when refunds are paid

Refunds are always made to the original payment method. We start processing within 1 working day. After that, the time for the money to reach you depends on your bank:

  • UPI: usually 1–3 working days
  • Debit / credit cards: usually 5–7 working days
  • Net-banking and wallets: usually 5–7 working days

For every refund on an invoiced booking we issue a GST credit note against your original tax invoice, available in your account.

8. Rescheduling

To change the time of a paid booking, cancel it and make a new booking; the refund rules in section 3 apply. When we offer a free reschedule under section 4, our support team will move the booking for you at no extra cost.

9. Questions and disputes

For any refund question, use the contact form and choose “Payment, refund or invoice”, or reach us directly. Please include your booking code. We aim to resolve refund requests within 7 working days.
